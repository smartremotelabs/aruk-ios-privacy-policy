Privacy Policy

TV Remote Control: Aruk for iPhone and iPad
Effective date: October 26, 2026

This policy describes how the iOS app TV Remote Control: Aruk (“the app”) handles information. The app is published by Smart Remote Labs. The person responsible is Thilina Sri Menikdiwela Hathurusinghege. Contact: smartremotelabs@gmail.com.

Summary

The app does not require an account. It does not include advertising or analytics software. It does not track you across other companies’ apps or websites. Information used to control your Roku stays on your iPhone or iPad, or is sent only to a Roku device on your local network. Voice search uses Apple’s speech recognition.

Information stored on your device

The app saves the following in local storage on your device so it can remember your setup:

  • The Roku you last connected to, including its name, local IP address, model name, and serial number, when the Roku provides them.
  • Your favorite channels and Quick Actions for that Roku.
  • Appearance, haptic feedback, and whether you finished the introductory screens.
  • How many free keyboard sessions you have used on the current day.
  • Whether the Pro upgrade is unlocked on this device, and whether the app has already asked for an App Store review.

This information is not uploaded to a Smart Remote Labs server. Deleting the app deletes this local copy.

Local network and your Roku

The app searches your Wi-Fi network for compatible Roku TVs and streaming devices and sends remote commands to the device you select. Those commands can include button presses, text you type, voice-search text, channel launches, and power commands. The app also reads device details, the active channel, the channel list, and channel icons from that Roku.

This traffic stays on your local network between your device and the Roku. The app needs Local Network permission for this to work. It does not use that permission to contact our servers.

Microphone and speech recognition

If you use voice search, the app uses the microphone to record what you say and Apple’s Speech framework to turn that speech into text. The text is then sent to your selected Roku, the same way typed text is sent.

The app does not keep a copy of the recording. It does not require on-device-only recognition, so Apple may receive the audio and recognized text to perform speech recognition, as described in Apple’s Speech Recognition disclosure. You can deny microphone or speech-recognition access in iOS Settings. Voice search will not work without those permissions, and the rest of the remote will still work.

Purchases

Pro is an optional one-time in-app purchase sold by Apple. Apple processes the payment. The app does not receive your card number, billing address, or Apple ID password. Apple tells the app whether this Apple ID has unlocked Pro. You can restore that purchase from Settings.

Apple’s handling of purchase information is described in Apple’s Privacy Policy.

App Store ratings

The app may ask iOS to show Apple’s review prompt after you have used the remote. Apple decides whether the prompt appears. If you choose Rate App in Settings, the app opens Apple’s review page when an App Store listing is available.

Advertising and tracking

This version of the iOS app does not show ads and does not use an advertising or analytics SDK. It does not collect identifiers for advertising, and it does not request permission to track you.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

How long information is kept

Local app data remains on your device until you delete the app or disconnect the saved Roku inside the app. Purchase records remain with your Apple ID and are controlled by Apple. Speech audio is not stored by the app.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this page with a new effective date. If a future version of the app adds advertising or other data collection, this policy will be updated before that version is released.
